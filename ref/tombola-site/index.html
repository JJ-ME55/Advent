The rules of the game

Every buy and every sell pays 6%. Where it goes:

4%Drop pot 1%Mega 0.7%Team 0.3%Launchpad = 6%Tax
  • 4%Drop pot
  • 1%Weekly mega
  • 0.7%Team

That is Tombola's 5% creator tax plus the launchpad's 1%. Prizes are paid in ETH. The tax is charged on the trade, not inside the token: sending tokens wallet to wallet is free.

  1. 1

    When. A drop every hour for the first 6 hours after launch, then every 4 hours. A mega drop once a week.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Who's out. The trading pool, the launch contracts and Tombola's own contracts sit out every draw. Everyone else holding is in.

    drand picks. The keeper commits to a future drand round, takes the snapshot, publishes the list, and the contract pays only a winner proven against that list. The signature is checked on-chain. No re-roll. Press Verify on any ticket and your browser re-does it.

  6. 6

    Hands off. No mint, no owner, no pause, no blacklist, no withdraw: the pots only ever leave as prizes. A cold guardian wallet can cancel a draw before it settles, and that's all it can do.
